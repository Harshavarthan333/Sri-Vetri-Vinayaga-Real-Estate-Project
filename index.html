Frequently Asked Questions.

Clear answers on land ownership, maintenance commitments, water security, and documentation.

What plot sizes are available at Sri Vetri Vinayaga real estate?
Plots are offered in 25 Cents, 50 Cents (0.50 Acre) and 1.00 Acre extents. Each plot is individually boundary-surveyed and pre-planted with bearing coconut Tress.
How is water guaranteed for every coconut plot?
Every parcel is connected to our multi-borewell grid through piped water conduits and pressurized drip lines that deliver moisture directly to each tree root system.
What is included in the One Year of Free Maintenance?
Our farm team handles ring de-weeding, organic farmyard compost application, drip irrigation checks, pest protection, and harvest collection for your first full year, at no extra charge.
What does the “Free EB Benefit” entail?
Each plot is prepared with a sanctioned agricultural electricity connection, step-down transformer access and underground cabling channels, so your farm power setup is ready from the start.
Are the land titles legally vetted and ready for registration?
Yes. Every plot comes with a 30-year parent document review, zero-encumbrance status, individual FMB sketch copies, government survey stone boundaries, and patta transfer support.
Can I schedule a guided site visit from Coimbatore?
Absolutely. Use the Book a Private Site Visit form below. Free transport from Coimbatore can be arranged, and we will share a legal kit preview during your visit.
